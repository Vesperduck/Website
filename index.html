The Artists

Meet the Band

01
VESPER
VOCALS · KEYS · RHYTHM GUITAR

The human heart of the band. Vesper writes from the liminal spaces — the edge of sleep, the last glow of a dying streetlight, the quiet between two bad decisions. His voice carries the weight of every city he’s walked through and every version of himself he’s left behind. He’s the storyteller, the spark, the one who turns ghosts into melodies.

02
VEX
BASSIST · CHAOS AGENT

All teeth and thunder. Vex plays bass like he’s trying to start a riot — low-end so thick it rewires your heartbeat, grooves that hit before you even know you’ve been hit. He’s the wildcard, the detonator, the one who grins while everything burns. Equal parts predator and performer.

03
KEPLER
DRUMS · SYNTH · CHAOS

Part myth, part machine. Kepler builds the storms the others walk through — synth layers that breathe, drums that hit like collapsing buildings, textures that feel alive. He’s the architect of the band’s universe, the one who turns emotion into circuitry and circuitry into sound.

04
DUCK
LEAD GUITAR · RIFF LORD

The one the whole band orbits. Duck plays guitar like he’s translating something ancient into electricity — riffs that lodge in your bones and solos that feel like a fuse being lit. Headphones always on, always listening, always three moves ahead. The sound of Vesper starts with him.
